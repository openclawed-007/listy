## Medium (~300 chars — meta description, Product Hunt, app listings)

CartLink is a free shared shopping list that syncs in real time across every phone in your household. Share it with a link or a QR code on the fridge — the people you live with don't need an account to tick things off or add to it. No ads, works offline, open source, native Android app.

## Long (~600 chars — About pages, press, directory descriptions)

Every shared shopping list app dies at the same step: getting everyone else in the house to download it and make an account. CartLink skips that step. You sign in with Google, add what you need, and turn on sharing. You get a link and a QR code you can stick on the fridge. Anyone who scans it sees the live list on their own phone and can tick things off or add to it — with no account at all, if you allow it. You stay in control: guests can never delete items, and you can switch sharing off whenever you like. It's free, there are no ads, it works offline in the basement supermarket, and there's a native Android app. The whole thing is open source.

## The "what it isn't" list (works as a closing paragraph anywhere)

No ads. No selling your data. No account for helpers. No mystery — the code's on GitHub.

## Feature phrasing (use these words, not the jargon)

| Say this | Not this |
|---|---|
| Tick it on one phone, it's gone on all of them | Real-time synchronisation |
| Stick the QR on the fridge | QR-code-based sharing |
| Helpers don't need an account | Anonymous authentication |
| Guests can never delete your list | Server-side permission enforcement |
| Keeps working in the basement supermarket | Offline-first architecture |
| Add "2" and "Dairy" and it groups by aisle | Quantity and category metadata |
| Undo, because thumbs slip | Soft delete with restore |
| A proper native Android app, no web view | Kotlin/Jetpack Compose client |
