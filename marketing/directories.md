# Directories and listings

Each of these is a permanent backlink and a small, steady trickle of the exact people searching for "shared shopping list". Most take five minutes. Do the top block first.

Use the **Short** or **Medium** blurb from [`taglines.md`](./taglines.md) and `public/og-image.jpg` as the image unless a size is specified.

## Do first (free, high value, low effort)

| Where | Why | Needs |
|---|---|---|

| [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/new) | The launch-day one. See `posts/product-hunt.md`. | Everything in that file |

## When it grows

- **Google Play Store** — the single biggest thing for Android adoption. $25 one-off. Everything the APK does today qualifies.
- **Wikipedia "Comparison of shopping list software"** — don't self-add; it'll be reverted. If a third party adds it, fine.
- **Local press / UK indie dev newsletters** — *Indie Hackers UK*, *The Indie Web*, *Console.dev* (they feature open-source dev tools weekly — the Firestore rules angle fits).
