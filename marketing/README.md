## The voice, in five rules

1. **Lead with the moment, not the feature.** "Did you say oat or almond?" beats "real-time sync".
2. **Say what it doesn't do.** No ads, no selling data, no account for helpers. People trust negatives.
3. **Be specific.** "Aisle four", "the fridge", "kitchen roll". Vague is forgettable.
4. **Never claim numbers you don't have.** No "loved by thousands". Zero users is fine; lying isn't.
5. **British English, short sentences, one joke per post maximum.**
