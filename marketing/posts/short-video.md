# 30-second video — TikTok / Reels / Shorts

The fridge QR is the most visual thing about CartLink. Film it for real: your kitchen, your fridge, a phone in hand. Vertical, 9:16. No music with lyrics. Captions on (most people watch muted).

Total: ~30 seconds. Cut fast. The first 2 seconds decide everything.

---

## Script

| Time | Shot | On-screen text | Voiceover (casual, not announcer) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Close-up: phone screen, a text thread. Bubble: *"did you say oat or almond??"* | **every household. every week.** | "Every household has this text." |
| 2–5s | Cut to: hand sticking a small printed QR code on a fridge door. Tape or magnet. | **so I put the list on the fridge** | "So I put the shopping list on the fridge." |
| 5–9s | Second person walks in, scans QR with their phone camera. Screen recording overlay: list appears instantly. | **no app. no account.** | "Anyone who scans it sees the live list. No app. No account." |
| 9–14s | Split screen: two phones. Left thumb ticks "Sourdough". Right phone: it strikes through with a little "Sam · just now" pill. | **tick it here → gone there** | "Tick it on one phone, it's gone on the other one before you've reached the next aisle." |
| 14–18s | Right phone types "Lemons", hits Add. Left phone shows "Lemons" appear. | **they can add. they can't delete.** | "They can add stuff. They can't delete your list. That's enforced properly, not just hidden." |
| 18–23s | Quick montage, ~1.2s each: list grouping by aisle · undo toast after a delete · dark mode toggle · the Android app icon on a home screen | **aisles · undo · dark mode · android** | "Aisles, undo, dark mode, proper Android app." |
| 23–27s | Back to the fridge, wide. QR visible. Phone held up showing the list. | **free. no ads. open source.** | "Free. No ads. The code's public." |
| 27–30s | Hold on the fridge. Big text. | **cartlink.co.uk** | "It's called CartLink." |

## Caption

```
The shopping list that lives on the fridge and in everyone's pocket. Scan it, tick it, done. No app, no account for the people helping. Free, no ads. cartlink.co.uk 🛒

#shoppinglist #grocerylist #lifehack #household #opensource #kitchenhacks
```

## Notes

- Use the real product. Don't mock it up in After Effects; the slight jank of a real screen recording is what makes people believe it.
- The "Sam · just now" moment is the emotional peak. Give it a full second.
- If you only have one phone, film the fridge scan and the tick with your phone, and use a laptop browser window for the "other person".
- Versions: cut a 15s version (shots 1, 2, 3, 4, 8) for Shorts and a 6s loop (shots 3 + 4) for a pinned profile clip.
- Post natively to each platform; cross-posted TikTok watermarks get suppressed on Reels.
